Focused, best of breed management

Consistent outperformance is one of the reasons clients choose Player Fund. Our practice acts for entrepreneurs and their families, companies and institutional clients, tailoring comprehensive solutions to the needs of a discerning clientele.

Our story

Player Fund was built on a simple premise: that a client's circumstances, not a shelf of standard products, should determine the solution. Early on, the practice chose to remain deliberately close to the entrepreneurs, families and institutions it served, so that every mandate could be handled with the care and continuity a long relationship requires.

That closeness has stayed the constant as the firm's reach has grown. Combining the classic values of an advisory firm with financial strength and broadly-based, in-depth expertise, Player Fund now provides its clients with market access and investment opportunities worldwide, while keeping the same hands-on relationship at the centre of the work.

Our values

Four commitments shape how we work with every client, regardless of the size or complexity of the mandate.

01

Discretion

Client affairs are handled with the confidentiality expected of a private advisory relationship, at every stage of a mandate.

02

Alignment

We act for our clients' interests specifically, declining engagements where that alignment cannot be maintained.

03

Rigour

Cross-asset expertise and a disciplined process sit behind every recommendation, not personal instinct alone.

04

Continuity

Relationships are built to last across market cycles, not around a single transaction or product.

Our philosophy

What does the client need, and what kind of solution can we offer to meet it, are the questions we ask before any other. We do not offer standard, one-size-fits-all solutions; the individually tailored questions are usually the ones that matter.

Cross-asset expertise is applied throughout the business, so a client is never far from the relevant specialism when it is time to make an important decision.

Transparency is a very important aspect of our work.

Our approach

We build the trust a long-term partnership demands by setting out clear, well-defined expectations from the outset, and revisiting them as a client's circumstances change.

A

Responsible ownership rights

An active, accountable approach to ownership across every holding we manage on a client's behalf.

B

Effective monitoring

Risk management across the full variety of investment factors bearing on a mandate, reviewed continuously rather than at fixed intervals.

Hands-on investment management

The investment management team applies a high level of diligence in the selection and ongoing monitoring of investments, informed by the deep interests of the company and the integrity of the market. Each investment and selection is the result of a fund's positioning relative to the wider mandate held by the management company.

A particular target investment has been selected by the investment management team in relation to equity or fixed-income investments in a particular sector, country or currency, so that the mandate can be determined against a genuinely relevant fundamental case.

Governance & risk management

Committees support the process, providing effective oversight, escalation and compliance with each investment mandate the firm holds.

01

Governance model

Decisions on behalf of clients pass through a defined committee structure before execution, providing independent scrutiny for every mandate.

02

Risk management

Risk modelling, aggregation and allocation is supported by real market data, evaluated and reported alongside every investment decision.

03

Operational management

Settlement, reconciliation and compliance with the operating mandate framework are managed to the same standard across every client relationship.

04

Dynamic threshold relationship

Highly selective about its investments, the management team adjusts its risk thresholds as a client's circumstances and market conditions evolve.
